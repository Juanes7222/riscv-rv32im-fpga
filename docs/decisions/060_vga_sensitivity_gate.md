# ADR 060 - Automated sensitivity of the VGA timing model

Status: Accepted, verified with Quartus Prime 25.1std.0.
Date: 2026-11-17.
Extends ADR 059 without changing its RTL or nominal constraints.

## Scope and objectives

The check supports the visualization objective and reliable counter readings
for the experimental comparison. It does not introduce a PCB characterization
objective, change the processor benchmarks or evaluate student learning.
Results are conditional model estimates; no external delay is labelled measured.

## Decision

After each replica is fitted, `scripts/vga_timing.py` invokes
`synthesis/report_vga_sensitivity.tcl` on that fixed placement and routing.
It evaluates slow/fast models at 0/85 C and 1100 mV for six symmetric external
skew budgets: 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 2.5 and 3 ns. Far-capacitance scenarios are the
project configuration and the four data/control-clock pairs 10/10, 10/20,
20/10 and 20/20 pF. These are deliberately hypothetical scenarios, not a
claimed range of DE1-SoC board parameters. Other board-model settings remain
at their project values, and the original explicit assignments are recorded.

The Cyclone V model ignores `OUTPUT_PIN_LOAD` as obsolete (warning 337001).
Use `BOARD_MODEL_FAR_C`, in farads, and repeat delay annotation with
`create_timing_netlist -force_dat`. Reject an ineffective capacitance sweep.
Do not accumulate output delays across scenarios. Do not export the temporary
assignments; restore the nominal delay annotation before returning.

Nominal acceptance requires all 25 DAC data/control endpoints in both setup
and hold, a forwarded DAC clock, zero unconstrained/illegal endpoints, no
ignored SDC, and nonnegative margins at +/-0.5 ns for the project and 10/10 pF
models. Global setup, hold and FPGA pulse-width checks must also pass at all
four corners. Recovery/removal reports can have no active paths under the
documented reset exceptions; zero paths are not a measured positive margin.
More severe sensitivity scenarios may fail and remain in the evidence.

The runner preserves the QPF/QSF and SOF bytes, refuses stale fits and nonempty
output directories, and archives inputs, summaries, raw reports and hashes.
Replica finalization and reuse require the VGA evidence to match its SOF,
source fingerprint and analysis version. Old replicas without this evidence
are regenerated by the existing campaign rather than silently adopted.

## Validation and references

Two fresh seed-5 candidates at revision `75d5c22` produced 120 cases each.
Both passed nominal acceptance. Eight Python checks exercise missing cases,
missing endpoints, failed nominal timing, ineffective loads, unconstrained
ports, nonfinite results and altered provenance. Six existing video/reset/CDC
benches are run by the full campaign before the experimental stages.

- [ADV7123 Rev. D](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/adv7123.pdf), Table 6, p. 8; Table 8, p. 10.
